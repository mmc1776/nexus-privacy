Nexus: Fall of Man - Delete your online data

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This page explains how to delete the data that the Nexus: Fall of Man app (the "App", published by MMC, on the App Store and Google Play) keeps for online multiplayer. See the Privacy Policy for what that data is.

What is stored online

For online multiplayer, Epic Online Services (EOS) keeps an anonymous device credential and a random player ID for your copy of the App. If you link a Google or Apple account to it, EOS also keeps that link. Lobbies and match connections exist only while a lobby or match is running. We do not run our own servers and keep no other copy.

Delete it in the App

If your version of the App has OPTIONS > GAMEPLAY > DELETE ONLINE ACCOUNT, use it. It deletes your online player ID and removes any linked Google or Apple account. A new player ID is created the next time you play online.

Delete it without the App

Uninstalling the App removes its device credential from your device; a new install gets a new, unrelated player ID. To have the data linked to your old player ID deleted at Epic, message us on our Discord at discord.gg/M73RJdwhx8 and say you want your Nexus: Fall of Man online data deleted. We will ask Epic to delete it and confirm when it is done.

What is kept

Your game progress and settings are saved only on your device and are not affected by deleting your online data. Uninstalling the App deletes them. We keep nothing else.

Sign in with Apple

On iPhone and iPad you can also stop using Sign in with Apple for the App in iOS Settings > your name > Sign in with Apple.
