Nexus: Fall of Man - Delete your online data

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This page explains how to delete the data that the Nexus: Fall of Man app (the "App", published by MMC (Marino Matthew Chesmore), on the App Store, Google Play and Steam) keeps for online multiplayer. See the Privacy Policy for what that data is.

What is stored online

  • Unity Gaming Services (Unity Technologies) keeps your online account: your Unity player ID and the Steam, Apple or Google account you signed in with, your display name, your friends, friend requests and blocked players, and a few account settings (the Online Rules version you accepted, when you last changed your name, extra blocked players) and a player-verification record holding your EOS player ID.
  • Epic Online Services (Epic Games) keeps a random player ID and its link to your Steam, Apple or Google account (older versions of the App also used an anonymous device credential).
  • Reports other players made about your display name, and reports you made, kept for up to 90 days after we act on them.

Lobbies, match connections and your online status exist only while you are online. We do not run our own servers and keep no other copy.

Delete it in the App

On a phone or tablet, open OPTIONS > GAMEPLAY > DELETE ONLINE ACCOUNT; on PC, open OPTIONS > GAMEPLAY > ONLINE NAME > ACCOUNT > DELETE ONLINE ACCOUNT. Then confirm. The App removes all your friends, friend requests and blocks, deletes your display name and account settings at Unity, deletes your Unity account, then deletes your EOS player ID and its sign-in link. On iPhone and iPad it also revokes the App's Sign in with Apple access. If the connection drops part-way, the App finishes the deletion the next time it starts. You cannot delete while you are in a lobby or a match. The next time you play online you start a new account with a new name.

Delete it without the App

Email mmcdev79509@gmail.com (or message us on our Discord at discord.gg/M73RJdwhx8) and say you want your Nexus: Fall of Man online data deleted. Tell us your display name with its #tag (for example Survivor#12345) and which platform you play on, so we can find your account; we may ask you to confirm the request from that account. We delete the Unity data ourselves, ask Epic to delete the EOS data, and confirm when it is done, within 30 days.

Uninstalling the App does not delete your online account (your name and friends come back when you sign in again); use one of the two ways above.

What is kept

Your game progress and settings are saved only on your device and are not affected by deleting your online data. Uninstalling the App deletes them. Display names in replays already saved on other players' devices stay there; we cannot reach those devices. We keep nothing else.

Sign in with Apple and Google

On iPhone and iPad you can also stop using Sign in with Apple for the App in iOS Settings > your name > Sign in with Apple. On Android you can remove the App's access from your Google account at myaccount.google.com/connections.
