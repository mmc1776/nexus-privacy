Nexus: Fall of Man - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

This policy covers the Nexus: Fall of Man app for iPhone and iPad (App Store) and for Android phones and tablets (Google Play) (together, the "App"), published by MMC.

The short version

The App has online multiplayer. To make it work, the App connects to Epic Online Services (EOS), a service run by Epic Games that we use for player sign-in, lobbies and match connections. EOS receives an anonymous device identifier and your connection details (such as your IP address). We do not ask for your name, email or any account, we do not run our own servers that store your data, and we never sell or share your data for advertising. Your progress and settings stay on your device.

What the App stores, and where

  • Game progress and settings (unlocked talents, match history, options such as language and controls) are saved only on your device. We cannot see them. Deleting the App deletes them.
  • Replays of your matches are saved only on your device. A replay of an online match includes the names the other players chose in that match.
  • The App does not ask for an account, your name, email, contacts, location, photos, camera, microphone or any other personal information.

Online multiplayer

Online play uses Epic Online Services (EOS), provided by Epic Games. When you start the App, it signs in to EOS anonymously so that multiplayer is ready when you want it. This is what is involved:

  • An anonymous device identifier. EOS creates a device credential for this copy of the App and a random player ID linked to it. It is not linked to your name, email, phone number or any other account, and we do not use it to identify you as a person or to track you.
  • Connection details. To host, find and join matches, your device exchanges network information (including your IP address) with Epic's servers. During a match, your game data travels to the other players either directly between devices or through Epic's relay servers, so the other players' devices may also see your IP address while you play together. Match data is used only to run the match.
  • Lobby name. If you host a game, the lobby name you type is visible to other players while the lobby exists. Do not put personal information in it.
  • Player pictures. To show each player's picture in the lobby and while connecting, the App shares the number of the Career portrait you have equipped with the other players in your lobby. If one of the other players is on the PC version and signed in to Steam, the App also receives that player's public Steam ID so it can show their Steam avatar. If you play the PC version through Steam, your public Steam ID is shared with the other players in your lobby, including players on phones, for the same purpose. It is not stored by the App and is not used for anything else.

Epic processes this data to provide the service, under the Epic Games privacy policy. We do not sell it or use it for advertising.

What the App does not do

  • No analytics or tracking. The App contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code, and does not track you across other apps or websites.
  • No advertising.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • Apart from the online play described above, no data is sent off your device by the App.

Apple and Google

You buy and download the App through the App Store or Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies; we never see your payment details.

iPhone and iPad: if you choose to share crash reports or app analytics with developers in your device settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous, aggregated crash and usage reports. This is controlled by you in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements, and is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Android: if you have turned on usage and diagnostics sharing on your device, Google may provide us with anonymous, aggregated crash and performance reports through Google Play Console. This is controlled by you in your Android or Google account settings, and is governed by Google's privacy policy.

Children

The App is rated for adults and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices and deleting your data

You can play every single-player mode without joining an online match. Deleting the App removes everything it stored on your device, including its EOS device credential; a new install gets a new, unrelated player ID. If you would like us to ask Epic to delete the data linked to your player ID, contact us using the details below.

Changes

If a future version changes what data is sent off your device (for example signing in with an Apple, Google or Steam account), we will update this policy, the App Store privacy details and the Google Play data safety section before that version is released, and change the date above.
