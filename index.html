Nexus: Fall of Man - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This policy covers the Nexus: Fall of Man app for iPhone and iPad (App Store) and for Android phones and tablets (Google Play) (together, the "App"), published by MMC.

The short version

The App does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal data. Everything you do in the game stays on your device.

What the App stores, and where

  • Game progress and settings (unlocked talents, match history, options such as language and controls) are saved only on your device. We cannot see them. Deleting the App deletes them.
  • The App does not ask for an account, your name, email, contacts, location, photos, camera, microphone or any other personal information.

What the App does not do

  • No analytics or tracking. The App contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code, and does not track you across other apps or websites.
  • No advertising.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • No data is sent off your device by the App.

Apple and Google

You buy and download the App through the App Store or Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies; we never see your payment details.

iPhone and iPad: if you choose to share crash reports or app analytics with developers in your device settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous, aggregated crash and usage reports. This is controlled by you in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements, and is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Android: if you have turned on usage and diagnostics sharing on your device, Google may provide us with anonymous, aggregated crash and performance reports through Google Play Console. This is controlled by you in your Android or Google account settings, and is governed by Google's privacy policy.

Children

The App is rated for adults and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version adds features that send data off your device (for example online multiplayer), we will update this policy, the App Store privacy details and the Google Play data safety section before that version is released, and change the date above.
