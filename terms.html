Nexus: Fall of Man - Online Rules (Terms of Use)

Version 1. Effective November 26, 2026.

These Online Rules are the terms of use for the online features of Nexus: Fall of Man (the "Game"): multiplayer, your display name, friends, invites, blocking and reporting. They are published by MMC (Marino Matthew Chesmore) ("we", "us"). Please read them to the end. By selecting ACCEPT you agree to them. If you do not agree, select DECLINE: you can still play every single-player mode.

1. How these rules fit with your store's terms

You got the Game from the App Store, Google Play or Steam, and that store's own terms still apply (for example Apple's Licensed Application End User License Agreement, the Google Play Terms of Service or the Steam Subscriber Agreement). These Online Rules add to those terms for online play. For anything about buying or licensing the Game, the store's terms come first. Online play uses services run by Epic Games (lobbies and match connections) and Unity Technologies (sign-in, display names and friends).

2. Who can play online

You must be at least 18 years old, and old enough where you live to agree to these rules. An account is for one person. Do not share, sell or trade your account, and do not use someone else's.

3. Your online account

Online play asks you to sign in: with Steam on PC, with Apple on iPhone and iPad, and with Google on Android. We never ask for your password; Apple, Google or Steam handles the sign-in. Your online account holds your display name, your friends, your friend requests, the players you blocked and the version of these rules you accepted. Keep your platform account secure: you are responsible for what happens on your account.

4. Display names

Other players see your display name in lobbies, matches, scoreboards, friends lists and saved replays. A #tag of digits is added so players with the same name can be told apart. Your name must not:

  • contain slurs or hate speech, or attack people for their race, ethnicity, religion, nationality, gender, sexual orientation, disability or age;
  • be sexual, obscene or threatening, or celebrate violence against real people;
  • harass, mock or target another player;
  • pretend to be us, a moderator, a platform (Steam, Apple, Google, Epic, Unity) or a real person;
  • contain personal information, such as a real name, phone number, address or social media account (yours or anyone else's);
  • advertise, contain a link, or promote cheats, account sales or other services;
  • refer to illegal drugs or other illegal activity;
  • use spelling tricks, symbols or look-alike letters to get around the name filter.

The Game checks every name with a filter, but a name the filter misses can still break these rules. We may hide or reset any name that breaks them, at any time and without notice; a reset name becomes "Survivor" followed by digits. After your first name, you can change it once every 7 days. These rules also apply to the lobby name you type when you host a game.

5. How you play

We have zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive players. Do not:

  • harass, threaten, bully or stalk anyone, in a match or outside it;
  • keep sending friend requests or invites to a player who has not accepted them, or make a new account to contact a player who blocked you;
  • cheat, use modified game files or third-party tools that give an unfair advantage, or exploit bugs on purpose;
  • disrupt matches, other players' connections or the online services, for example by flooding them with traffic;
  • file false reports or misuse REPORT NAME;
  • break the law, or help anyone else break it.

The Game has no text or voice chat. Friend requests and invites are the only messages players can send, and they carry no text.

6. Friends, invites and what friends see

You can add a friend with their friend code (Name#12345), from your recent players, or from a lobby or scoreboard. Your friends can see when you are online, whether you are in a lobby or a match, and which platform you play on, and they can join your lobby when it is open to them. To hide this, turn on APPEAR OFFLINE in the friends panel. Your list of recent players is saved only on your device. The online service limits list sizes (for example 50 friends); when a list is full, remove someone to add someone new.

7. Blocking and reporting

You can block any player from the friends panel, a lobby seat or a scoreboard row. A blocked player cannot send you friend requests or invites or see you online, and their lobbies are hidden from you. You can unblock them in the BLOCKED tab.

You can report a player's name with REPORT NAME in the same places. A report sends us the reported player's online IDs, the name you saw and the reason you picked; there is no free text. We review reports and act on those that break these rules, normally within 24 hours. To protect everyone's privacy, we may not tell you what action we took.

8. What happens if you break these rules

Depending on how serious it is, we may reset your display name, remove you from a lobby or match, limit your use of friends and invites, or suspend or permanently ban your account from online play. You can still play every single-player mode. If you think we made a mistake, contact us at mmcdev79509@gmail.com and we will look again.

9. Deleting your online account

You can delete your online account at any time in OPTIONS > GAMEPLAY > DELETE ONLINE ACCOUNT on a phone or tablet (on PC: OPTIONS > GAMEPLAY > ONLINE NAME > ACCOUNT > DELETE ONLINE ACCOUNT). This deletes your display name, friends, friend requests, blocked players and the other online records the Game keeps for your account, and unlinks your sign-in. Your progress and settings on your device are not affected. You can also ask us to delete it without the Game: see https://mmc1776.github.io/nexus-privacy/delete.html.
